Core Features

Everything a dashcam needs

Full surround dashcam functionality using only BYD's built-in cameras — no extra hardware required.

🎥

4-Channel 360° Recording

Records all four cameras (front, rear, left, right) simultaneously to H.264 MP4. Both individual streams and a 2×2 composite are saved.

📍

GPS & Telemetry Overlay

Burns speed (km/h & mph), gear (P/R/N/D), turn signals, accelerator/brake depth, and headlight state into video and live preview. Battery %, driving range, energy mode (EV/HEV), and drive mode (ECO/SPT) are also displayed. GPX tracks are saved alongside video.

🛡

Parking Guard — Triple Detection

Three detection methods protect your parked car: accelerometer impact (default 2.5G), camera motion (levels 1–5), and BYD's 8 radar proximity sensors (configurable GREEN/YELLOW/RED trigger). Each triggers instant locked recording.

⏪

Event Pre-buffer

Maintains a 12-second sliding pre-buffer while parked. When an impact or motion event fires, the segment recorded before the event is locked together with the event segment — ensuring you never lose the moments leading up to an incident.

🔄

Continuous Loop Recording

Records in configurable segments (default 3 min) and automatically deletes the oldest segments when storage is full. Locked event segments are always protected.

📱

Remote Phone Access

Connect from your iOS or Android phone over the same Wi-Fi to view a live camera preview, browse and download recordings, and toggle parking guard. Cloudflare Tunnel enables access from anywhere.

🔔

Telegram Alerts

Receive instant Telegram notifications on impact or motion events. Just enter your Bot Token and Chat ID — no server setup needed.

🏠

MQTT & Home Assistant

Publishes GPS, battery, and event data to any MQTT broker. Supports Home Assistant MQTT Discovery so sensors register automatically.

⚡

Ignition-based Auto-switching

Ignition ON (PowerLevel≥2) immediately exits parking guard and resumes recording. Ignition OFF (ON→ACC) immediately enters parking guard — no 30-second delay. Powered by BYDAutoBodyworkDevice polling.

How it works

Up and running in 4 steps

No hardware installation required — just install the APK and go.

1

Install the APK

Download the latest APK from the Releases page and sideload it onto the BYD infotainment system.

2

Grant Permissions

Allow Camera and Location permissions on first launch. Configure storage path, segment length, and other preferences.

3

Recording Starts Automatically

Recording begins when the car starts. Enable parking guard and the app watches over your car while parked — switching modes instantly on ignition changes.

4

Check from Your Phone

Connect the phone app over Wi-Fi to view the live preview and event recordings. Telegram alerts notify you the moment an incident occurs.
